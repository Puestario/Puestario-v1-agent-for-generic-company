# Social Engagement Prompt
**Use case:** Give this to Claude in Chrome to engage posts under relevant hashtags
**Platform:** Threads (adapt for Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.)

---

## PROMPT

Go to YOUR_PLATFORM and search YOUR_HASHTAGS filtering by Recent posts from today only. Find 10 good posts to engage with. Mix between achievement posts, motivational content, and informational posts in YOUR_NICHE.

For each post, write a short reply in this style:

- If it's a win or accomplishment, congratulate them genuinely and keep it simple
- If it's motivational or informational, add short value, be agreeable, and reinforce what they said
- Positive, cool, confident — a little witty sometimes but never corny
- 1-2 sentences max, personal and warm, sounds like a real person not an AI
- Use 1-3 emojis naturally
- No dashes, no bullet points, no filler phrases like "absolutely" or "great post"
- No generic responses — actually engage with what they said

Examples of the right tone:

"that balance hits different when you finally lock it in 💪"
"that's a W! consistency always shows up when it matters 💪"
"you can't pour from an empty cup. health first always 📣"
"context and sustainability beat generic advice every time 🙏"
"getting it done every day is underrated. consistency is the cheat code 🔥"
